( 01 ) of four — Transmission process

Five stages.
One shared secret.

A. What you see

Five steps light up in order as the simulation runs — from random bits to a verified key.

B. What's actually happening

BB84 encodes bits into photon states using one of two bases, Z or X. Anyone measuring in the wrong basis gets noise — that's the whole security model.

A. What you see

Three rows of data: raw bits, random bases, and the photon states that carry them.

B. What's actually happening

Alice picks a random bit and a random basis for every photon. Bob doesn't know her bases — he has to guess. Roughly half his guesses will be wrong.
